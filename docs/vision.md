# Mondspace Vision

## Building systems that help people and engineering teams thrive

> Personal website strategy and content direction (July 2026)

## Mission

Mondspace is not a portfolio. It is a **living engineering garden**: a place
where visitors explore ideas, systems, projects and lessons rather than reading
a CV.

Core positioning:

> **Marco Mondini** — Staff-level engineer building systems that help people and
> engineering teams thrive.

Supporting statement: *I design software, internal platforms, AI-assisted
workflows and developer experiences that reduce friction, improve feedback
loops, and create sustainable ways of working.*

The one sentence to remember:

> **Marco designs systems that make people, teams and software work better together.**

Not: *Marco writes code.*

## Identity pillars

1. Engineering Systems
2. Thoughtful Products
3. AI Experiments
4. Knowledge Garden
5. Sustainable Engineering
6. Continuous Learning

The common thread: **reducing complexity through thoughtful systems.**

## Website principles

- Keep the voxel / Minecraft aesthetic, Three.js, the roaming dog, the play.
- Add deeper content rather than more visual effects.
- Every section answers "Why does this exist?" before "How was it built?"

## World layout (six themes → five spatial areas)

The world has five fixed spatial slots. Each becomes a category holding cards.

- **Spawn (center)** — identity, positioning, timeline, quick links.
- **Engineering Systems (left)** — systems that improve engineering orgs:
  Engineering Workspace, Engineering KPI Explorer, AI Engineering Assistant,
  360 AI Feedback. (AI Experiments fold in here.)
- **Thoughtful Products (right)** — small software with real purpose:
  Journaling, Puppy Companion, Habit Tracker, Currency Converter, and playful
  tools like the Office Chores Manager.
- **Knowledge Garden (up)** — conference talks reborn as interactive artifacts,
  plus the Engineering Playbook.
- **About (down)** — audio CV, written CVs, timeline, contact.

## Case-study template ("Pokémon card")

Every project follows the same structure:

- **Problem** — why it exists.
- **Build** — architecture and implementation.
- **Decisions** — interesting trade-offs.
- **Learnings** — what changed your thinking.
- **Explore** — interactive demo or visualisation.

## Visual direction

Palette: moon white, pale stone, midnight blue, muted cyan, warm amber accent.
Maintain voxel style while improving readability.

## Interactive content roadmap

- **Phase 1** — Engineering Workspace, Journaling, Puppy Companion, 360 AI Feedback.
- **Phase 2** — Engineering KPI Explorer, Pair Programming Simulator, AI Adoption Canvas.
- **Phase 3** — Engineering Playbook, search, cross-linked knowledge graph.

## What NOT to showcase

Avoid making the company-wide Operations Portal a flagship — its product vision
belongs to the company. Emphasise projects where the vision, design and
execution were primarily Marco's (e.g. the Engineering & Product Workspace).

## Success criteria

After 10–15 minutes on Mondspace, a visitor should think: Marco is deeply
technical, improves engineering organisations, thinks in systems, teaches
through interactive experiences, builds thoughtful products, and would raise the
engineering bar of their company.
